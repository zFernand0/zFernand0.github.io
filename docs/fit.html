The Golden Rules for Abs & Muscle Retention

1. Keep Protein High (150g+)

In a caloric deficit, high protein prevents muscle breakdown and keeps hunger hormones down.

2. Direct Abs Work 2-3x/Week

Perform progressive heavy core work (Cable Crunches, Weighted Planks, Hanging Raises) to build abdominal wall thickness.

3. Prioritize Daily Steps over Intense Cardio

8,000–10,000 steps daily burns fat without over-fatiguing your nervous system or increasing ravenous appetite.

4. Patient Consistency

Aim for 0.5 to 1.5 lbs per week of weight loss. Faster loss often causes muscle wasting.
